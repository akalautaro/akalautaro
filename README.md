## About

I'm a Data Engineer. I've mostly worked at consultancies, on data projects for clients across different industries, and I also have product experience. I build complete data platforms, from the moment data comes in until someone uses it to make a decision, and I aim for them to be clear, reliable, and easy to evolve.

I started in technical support, working close to accounting, sales, and logistics, and began automating repetitive tasks with Python, SQL, and APIs. Today I'm at Blueprintdata, building end-to-end data stacks, semantic layers, and analytics agents for clients.

## Selected impact

- Led Data Engineering & Analytics at Decentraland: cut Snowflake BI credit usage by ~44% and p95 query latency by ~95%, removed 30% of redundant dbt models, and reduced the data SLA from 3 hours to 45 minutes.
- Designed a MetricFlow semantic layer covering 30+ KPIs, with an LLM agent on top for natural-language analytics.
- Automated a Big Data process for Coca-Cola FEMSA across 5 LATAM markets with Databricks (PySpark), from 4 hours of manual work to 5 minutes.
